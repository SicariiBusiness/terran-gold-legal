Terran Gold — Refund Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

All in-app purchases — including Terran Credits packs (Vein, Rich Vein, Mother Lode, Colossal Cache, Kingsmine), Founder's Fortune starter pack, and Subscriptions (Basic, Premium) — are processed through Google Play Billing and are subject to Google Play's refund policy. Terran Gold LLC does not process refunds directly for these purchases.

To request a refund for a Google Play purchase:

  1. Visit play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory
  2. Find the transaction you want refunded
  3. Click Report a problem → I'd like to request a refund
  4. Follow Google's prompts

Google's standard refund window is 48 hours from purchase for automatic refunds, with case-by-case review beyond that period. Google Play's full refund policy: support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637

2. Terran Credits & Virtual Items

Terran Credits are a virtual in-app currency with no real-world value outside of Terran Gold. They are used exclusively for purchasing properties, upgrades, and in-game bonuses within the app. Terran Credits are non-transferable and cannot be exchanged for real money.

Once Terran Credits have been spent within the app (on properties, upgrades, or other items), those transactions are final and non-refundable.

3. Founder's Fortune Starter Pack

Founder's Fortune is a one-time promotional pack available only to new users during their first 7 days after installing the app. It is marketed as non-refundable at time of purchase. Once claimed, the contents (Founder tiles, gold, boost bank, Founder badge) are permanently credited to your account and cannot be reversed.

4. Subscriptions (Basic & Premium)

Basic ($4.99/month) and Premium ($9.99/month) subscriptions renew automatically through Google Play Billing. You can cancel at any time via:

play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions

Upon cancellation, you retain access to subscription benefits through the end of your current paid billing period. Partial-period refunds are handled per Google's refund policy — see Section 1 above.

5. PayPal Cashouts

Cashouts from your in-game gold balance to your PayPal account are one-way and final. Once a cashout has been submitted and processed by PayPal Payouts API, the gold cannot be re-credited to your Terran Gold account.

If a cashout fails due to an incorrect PayPal email or account issue on your end, the gold will be re-credited automatically. Please double-check your PayPal email address before submitting a cashout.

6. Chargebacks & Fraud

Fraudulent chargebacks or reversal attempts on in-app purchases may result in permanent suspension of your Terran Gold account and forfeiture of any remaining gold, Terran Credits, and properties. If you believe a charge was made in error, please contact us at the email below before initiating a chargeback so we can attempt to resolve it directly.

7. Contact

For refund-related questions or disputes, please email:

support.terrangold.app@gmail.com

(Or use the "Contact Support" link inside the app under Profile → Help & Support.)

We aim to respond to all support inquiries within 3 business days.
