2 · Legal basis (GDPR / UK-GDPR)

  • Contract — running the game, processing purchases and cashouts.
  • Legal obligation — tax reporting, KYC/AML, responding to lawful requests.
  • Legitimate interest — fraud prevention, security, service improvement, anonymised analytics.
  • Consent — precise location, personalised ads, marketing emails (opt-in only). Withdrawable at any time in Profile → Privacy or by contacting the DPO.

3 · Who we share with

  • Google (Sign-In, Firebase, AdMob): account identity, ad targeting, crash reports. Google is a separate controller for advertising data.
  • Apple (App Store): only for iOS purchase receipts.
  • RevenueCat: processor for in-app-purchase entitlement management.
  • PayPal: recipient email + payout amount when you cash out. PayPal is a separate controller for its own KYC.
  • MongoDB Atlas / hosting providers: processors under a data-processing agreement.
  • Emergent Deployments (Kubernetes hosting): processor.
  • Tax / legal advisors, when required: only the minimum necessary data.
  • Law-enforcement, when compelled: only under a valid legal request.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share your data with data brokers.

4 · International transfers

Our servers are hosted primarily in the United States. When we transfer data out of the EU/UK/EEA, we rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (where the recipient is certified) and the UK Extension. Standard Contractual Clauses (2021/914) are executed with all sub-processors that are not DPF-certified. A copy of the SCCs applicable to your data is available on request.

5 · Your rights

EU / UK / EEA / IL / CH residents

  • Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection.
  • Withdraw consent at any time (does not affect prior lawful processing).
  • Lodge a complaint with your national data-protection authority (list at edpb.europa.eu). Israel: PPA — gov.il/privacy-protection-authority.

California / Colorado / Connecticut / Utah / Virginia residents (US)

  • Right to know what we collect, right to delete, right to correct, right to opt out of "sharing / sale" (we don't sell — but the opt-out button is available anyway in Profile → Privacy for CCPA compliance).
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising any of the above.
  • Authorised-agent requests accepted with proof of authorisation.

Everyone else

You have the right to access and correct your data at any time from Profile → Account, and to delete your account. Non-account data (server logs, analytics) is anonymised or purged per §1 retention.

6 · How to exercise rights

Email support.terrangold.app@gmail.com with subject "Data Subject Request" and include:

  • Which right you want to exercise (access / delete / etc.)
  • The email address linked to your Terran Gold account
  • Enough info for us to verify identity (last purchase date, recent tile claim, etc.)

We respond within 30 days (GDPR/UK-GDPR), 45 days (CCPA, extendable once by another 45 days if we tell you why).

7 · Children

The App is not directed at children under 18. If we learn we have collected data from a user under 18 (or under 21 where local law applies to cashouts), we will delete the account and refund any pending cashout.

8 · Security

  • TLS 1.2+ for all traffic between the App and our servers.
  • Encryption at rest for the MongoDB Atlas cluster.
  • Least-privilege server access; audited admin actions (mint / cashout override) require a rotating ADMIN_KEY.
  • Server-side receipt validation for every IAP.
  • Region + age verification prior to first cashout.

No system is perfectly secure. In the event of a breach affecting your personal data we will notify you and the applicable supervisory authority within 72 hours of becoming aware, per GDPR Art. 33/34 (or the local equivalent).

9 · Cookies & SDKs

The App itself does not use browser cookies. It does embed the following native SDKs:

  • Google Sign-In (Emergent-managed)
  • Google AdMob (rewarded + banner ads; user can opt out of personalisation via device settings)
  • RevenueCat (IAP entitlement)
  • Expo (crash logging, over-the-air updates)
