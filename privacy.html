Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 15, 2026

1. Who We Are

Terran Gold is an idle mobile game published by Terran Gold Studio. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

2. Data We Collect

  • Account: email address, display name, and profile picture (via Google Sign-In).
  • Gameplay: gold balance, Terran Credits, owned tiles, streak, and event participation.
  • Approximate location: only to reveal geo-tiles near you. We never store your precise coordinates on our servers — only the coarse cell grid you selected.
  • Advertising ID (Android) / IDFA (iOS): only with your consent, used by Google AdMob for rewarded ads.
  • Purchases: RevenueCat transaction receipt IDs for anti-fraud reconciliation. We do not store card, Google Play, or Apple Pay details.

3. How We Use It

  • Run gameplay and calculate rewards.
  • Fulfil PayPal payouts you request.
  • Detect duplicate ad-reward and IAP claims.
  • Fix bugs, prevent fraud, and improve the game.

4. Advertising

Rewarded ads are served by Google AdMob. On iOS 14+ we ask for your permission via the App Tracking Transparency prompt before Google can use your IDFA for personalization. On Android you can reset or disable your Ad ID in your device settings.

5. In-App Purchases

Terran Credit packs are consumable in-app purchases processed by Apple, Google, or via RevenueCat. Prices vary by country. All purchases are non-refundable per Apple/Google policies except where required by law.

6. Sharing

We share limited data with:

  • Google (Sign-In, AdMob).
  • RevenueCat (purchase validation).
  • PayPal (only when you initiate a cashout — your PayPal email is passed through so PayPal can pay you).

We do not sell your personal information.

7. Data Retention & Your Rights

Your account and gameplay data are kept while your account is active. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from Profile → Danger Zone → Delete Account, or by following the steps at Account Deletion. This action is irreversible and typically completes within 30 days. If you are in the EU/UK (GDPR) or California (CCPA/CPRA) you also have the right to access, correct, and export your data — email us at support.terrangold.app@gmail.com.

8. Children

Terran Gold is not directed to children under 13. If we learn we have collected data from a child under 13 without parental consent, we will delete it.

9. Contact

Questions or requests? Reach us at support.terrangold.app@gmail.com.
